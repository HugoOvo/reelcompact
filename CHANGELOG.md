# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-06

- Initial public release.
- Finder Quick Actions for H.264 and H.265.
- 19.5 MiB hard target with adaptive retries.
- VMAF-assisted resolution selection.
- HDR-to-SDR conversion and AAC audio budgeting.
- Native SwiftUI progress and cancellation window.
- Self-contained ARM64 DMG with bundled FFmpeg.
